Accessibility

%OURS_DRAFT_NOTE%

Ours should work well for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, keyboard, switch access, zoom, or who see colour differently.

What we aim for

  • WCAG 2.2 at level AA across the app, checked with automated tests on every change.
  • The two partners’ colours are tested against the common types of colour blindness, and whose plan it is is also given in words: in each plan’s details, in the key under the plan, and to screen readers. If the colours are still hard to tell apart, turn on “Tell us apart by shape too” in Privacy → Appearance, and each partner gets a shape as well (a circle and a square).
  • Everything can be done with a keyboard, with visible focus and a skip link.
  • Text reflows down to 320 pixels wide and up to 200% zoom, and the app follows your system's text size, dark mode and reduced-motion settings.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels.

Known limitations

  • The week view is a visual summary. The agenda list next to it carries the same information in a form that works better with screen readers.
  • Calendars imported from other services keep whatever titles those services provide.

Tell us

If something gets in your way, please tell us at the contact above or by opening an issue on the source repository. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
