%OURS_DRAFT_NOTE%

Ours is built so that the people running it cannot read what you and your partner plan. Your plans, lists, comments and check-ins are encrypted on your devices before they leave them, with keys only your devices hold. This page lists everything the server does see.

What we cannot see

  • The content of anything you write: titles, notes, places, times of plans, list items, comments, check-in answers, names you give each other.
  • Your encryption keys and your 12-word recovery phrase. We store your key backup only in a form sealed with that phrase. If you let a passkey unlock your keys, we also store a copy sealed with a key only that passkey can produce, after your face, fingerprint or device passcode.
  • The contents of calendars you import from a file.

Optional features that reveal more

  • Calendar subscription links. If you subscribe to an outside calendar by link, our server fetches it for you, so it sees that link and that calendar's contents while passing them on. They are not stored or logged. Importing a file instead keeps everything on your device.
  • Reminders and notifications. To send a reminder while your device is asleep, the server must know when it fires and which device to wake, and it sees when you and your partner notify each other. The text stays encrypted; not even the push service can read it.
  • Calendar feed for other apps. If you turn this on, your busy times (start and end only) are stored unencrypted so your calendar app can read them. Turning it off deletes them.

What we never do

  • No analytics, trackers or advertising. Fonts and code are served from this site; the only outside script is the optional bot check on the sign-in page.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There are no investors to answer to; the software is free.
  • Request logs record only the route, status and timing, never IP addresses, request bodies or tokens. The hosting platform's own request logs are switched off.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare hosts the server and database (%OURS_REGION%). Like any network, it sees IP addresses in transit.
  • Our email provider (%OURS_EMAIL_PROVIDER%) sees your email address and when we send you a link.
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) carries encrypted notifications and sees when they are sent.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files and sees the usual web request details.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, if this instance uses it, checks the sign-in page for bots. It runs only on that page and sees what a web request shows (your IP address and browser); it does not use cookies to track you.

Your choices and rights

  • Export everything you can see, at any time, from Privacy in the app.
  • Delete your account from Privacy in the app. It takes effect immediately: your account, devices, private records and key backup are deleted, and shared records you created stay with your partner, with your name removed. Backups of the database roll off within 30 days.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, at the contact above. Because we cannot read your content, the table above is the complete answer.
  • If you are in the UK or EU you can complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Ours is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes
